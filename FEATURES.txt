TOWNSHIP EXPANSION
Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3. Needs Fabric API and Java 25. Works in existing worlds.
Source: https://github.com/Plut0ast/minecraft_township_expansion

Villages become real towns with a Town Center, and nitwits can be armed as Sentries to guard
them. Every villager has a name, and every town has an opinion of you: earn its trust and it
opens up. Everything else about villagers (jobs, trading, breeding) stays vanilla.


TOWNS
-----
- Every village gets a Town Center by its bell. You can also craft your own and found a
  town anywhere.
- Each town gets a name, like Oakhollow or Ravenmere.
- Villagers and iron golems within 64 blocks join the closest Town Center. When a
  villager joins, it walks to the Town Center and celebrates.
- Placing a Town Center tells you what you founded and how far away the nearest other
  town is.
- Breaking a Town Center frees its villagers, and they look for another town.

Town Center recipe:
    [      ] [ Map     ] [       ]
    [Diamond] [ Planks  ] [Diamond]
    [Planks ] [ Planks  ] [Planks ]

Joining and extra Town Centers:
You can only be a player of one town. Trusted players can join a town at its Town Center.
Placing a Town Center while you are in a town adds it to your town (its area grows); to
found a new town, use Leave at your Town Center first.

Town relations:
Browsing another town at your Town Center, Trusted players can set how your town treats it:
  Enemy    Your villagers hide from its members; your golems and Sentries attack them.
  Neutral  Your golems and Sentries only fight back if hit (the default).
  Good     Your golems and Sentries never attack them.
Members means everyone in that town: villagers, Sentries, golems and its players. Each town
sets its own stance.

Ringing a bell makes the town's Town Center glow for a few seconds, through walls.

Town banners:
Trusted players can put a banner in the slot next to the town's name. Everyone browsing
towns sees a small version of it on that town's page. Breaking the Town Center drops it.
The town's villagers, Sentries and players all wear its banner as a cape. Turning off
"Cape" in Skin Customization hides yours.

Town alerts:
A town's players get a message above the hotbar when one of its villagers dies (and what
killed it), a raid starts there, or an enemy player arrives.

The Town Center screen shows:
- Your rank with the town, with a progress bar showing how far you are from the next rank.
- Everyone who lives there, listed as Players, Villagers (with their jobs), Sentries and
  Golems. Click a heading to fold that list away.
- Arrows to browse every other town in the dimension, and a filter that shows only towns
  with players.
- Trusted players can rename the town and join it, and Trusted members can remove
  villagers from it.


REPUTATION
----------
Each town remembers you separately. You earn reputation with a town by:
  - trading with its villagers (+1 a trade)
  - killing hostile mobs in its area (+1, up to 10 a day)
  - helping win a raid on it (+50)
  - curing a zombie villager that then joins it (+15)
Hitting one of its villagers costs 5, and killing one costs another 25.

  Rank       Reputation    What it unlocks
  ---------  ------------  ------------------------------------------------------
  Enemy      -10 or less   Iron golems and Sentries attack you
  Stranger   -9 to 9       You can still feed Sentries
  Visitor    10+
  Friend     40+           Rename villagers
  Trusted    120+          Give orders, rename the town, arm nitwits, change villagers'
                           armour and Sentries' gear, join the town, Sentries guard you
  Hero       300+          Cheaper trades with every villager in the town
The town's players can always change its villagers' armour and its Sentries' gear.


VILLAGERS
---------
- Every villager has a name, and a Friend can rename it.
- Right-click to trade as usual. Shift-right-click to open its page: health, job, town
  and armour.
- Armour shows on the villager, protects it, and wears out like yours.
- Orders (Trusted): Wandering, Staying, Following. Followers go back to Wandering if the
  player they follow logs off. Followers never get left behind (flying, pearls,
  teleports) and come with you through portals to other dimensions.
- When a villager dies, it drops everything it carried.
- Jobs, trading and breeding are vanilla (apart from more nitwits, below).


NITWITS & SENTRIES
------------------
- Give a nitwit a sword, axe, spear, bow or crossbow (you need to be Trusted) and it
  becomes a Sentry. Sentries fight hostile mobs in their town and attack the town's
  Enemies. Archers shoot arrows from their pockets; crossbow Sentries load and fire like
  pillagers.
- A Sentry that follows a Trusted player becomes their bodyguard.
- A nitwit's page also shows its 8 pockets (where archers keep arrows) and a food slot.
  Below half health it eats from the food slot, then goes back to its weapon. The food
  slot is separate from its inventory, so it never affects breeding.
- Nitwit babies need a nitwit parent: 50% with one nitwit parent, 100% with two. Two
  normal villagers never have a nitwit. Spawn eggs give a nitwit 20% of the time.
- Iron golems get names like Rivet-42, join towns, and go after the town's Enemies.


COMMANDS
--------
For operators, or in single-player with cheats on. Tab completes town names, and
"nearest" picks the closest town.

  /town list
  /town reputation get <town> [players]
  /town reputation set <town> <players> <amount>
  /town reputation add <town> <players> <amount>
  /town reputation rank <town> <players> hero
  /town reload                      re-reads the settings file


SETTINGS
--------
The file config/townsfolk.properties is created the first time the game starts.
Set any option to false to turn that feature off (for example sentries=false).
Sentries (weapons, range, damage, cooldowns, bodyguards), capes, alerts, Town Center
generation, nitwit chances and the reputation amounts can be changed. Use /town reload
or restart to apply.


Township Expansion is still in development, so expect changes.
