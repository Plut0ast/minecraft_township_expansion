TOWNSHIP EXPANSION
Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3. Needs Fabric API and Java 25. Works in existing worlds.
Source: https://github.com/Plut0ast/minecraft_township_expansion

Villages become real towns. Every villager has a name, a job they actually do, and an
opinion of you. Trade with a town to earn its trust, and it will open up: give orders,
rename it, arm its guards, and join it as one of its own.


TOWNS
-----
- Every village gets a Town Center by its bell. You can also craft your own and found a
  town anywhere.
- Each town gets a name, like Oakhollow or Ravenmere.
- Villagers and iron golems within 64 blocks join the newest Town Center nearby. When a
  villager joins, it walks to the Town Center and celebrates.
- Placing a Town Center tells you what you founded and how far away the nearest other
  town is.
- Breaking a Town Center frees its villagers, and they look for another town.
- Every village farm gets a chest with a few starter seeds.

Town Center recipe:
    [      ] [ Map     ] [       ]
    [Diamond] [ Planks  ] [Diamond]
    [Planks ] [ Planks  ] [Planks ]

Town banners:
Trusted players can put a banner in the slot next to the town's name. Everyone browsing
towns sees a small version of it on that town's page. Breaking the Town Center drops it.
The town's villagers, Sentries and players all wear its banner as a cape. A player in
several towns wears the nearest one's banner. Turning off "Cape" in Skin Customization
hides yours.

The Town Center screen shows:
- Your rank with the town, with a progress bar showing how far you are from the next rank.
- Everyone who lives there, listed as Players, Villagers (with their jobs) and Golems.
- Arrows to browse every other town in the dimension, and a filter that shows only towns
  with players.
- Trusted players can rename the town, Heroes can join it, and Trusted members can remove
  villagers from it.


REPUTATION
----------
Each town remembers you separately. Every trade with one of its villagers earns +1.
Hitting one of its villagers costs 5, and killing one costs another 25.

  Rank       Reputation    What it unlocks
  ---------  ------------  ------------------------------------------------------
  Enemy      -10 or less   Iron golems and Sentries attack you
  Stranger   -9 to 9       You can still feed villagers
  Visitor    10+
  Friend     50+           Rename villagers
  Trusted    150+          Give villagers orders, rename the town, arm nitwits
  Hero       400+          Join the town, and Sentries guard you
Only the town's players can change its villagers' gear and pockets (Sentries too).
You can only be a player of one town: joining or founding another one leaves your old town.


VILLAGERS
---------
- Every villager has a name, and a Friend can rename it.
- Right-click to trade as usual. Shift-right-click to open its page: health, job, town,
  armour, food and pockets.
- Armour shows on the villager, protects it, and wears out like yours.
- Food: below half health, a villager takes food from its pockets into its hand and eats
  it to heal, then goes back to holding what it had. (The optional food slot setting adds
  starting bread and trips to farmers and butchers for more.)
- Orders (Trusted): Wandering, Staying, Following, and Farming for farmers.
- Villagers with worn-out gear walk to the right smith to get it repaired.

Trades and your standing:
Every villager rerolls its trades each Minecraft morning. It also remembers how much YOU
have traded with it. That standing runs from Novice to Master, unlocks more of its trades
for you, and carries over between rerolls.


JOBS
----
Villagers work on their own during the day, near their workstation.

  Farmer         When ordered to farm: harvests, replants, and stores crops in the
                 nearest chest
  Fisherman      Fishes at nearby water and keeps the catch in its barrel
  Shepherd       Gets a fenced pen with three sheep, shears them, and stores the wool
  Butcher        Smokes raw meat from its chest and feeds hungry villagers
  Cleric         Heals hurt villagers nearby
  Fletcher       Crafts arrows from flint, sticks and feathers, and keeps archers supplied
  Armorer        Repairs armour and kits Sentries out in chainmail and iron
  Leatherworker  Repairs leather armour and gives other villagers leather armour
  Weaponsmith    Repairs weapons and hands out iron hoes, axes and shears
  Toolsmith      Repairs tools, including any left in its chest, for an emerald each
  Mason, Librarian, Cartographer: coming later

Get your gear repaired:
Smiths have a Repair button next to their trade screen. Put in your damaged item and some
emeralds, and it comes back repaired with its enchantments kept.
  1 emerald:  leather, wood or gold
  2 emeralds: stone, chainmail or iron
  4 emeralds: diamond or netherite armour
  6 emeralds: netherite tools


NITWITS, SENTRIES & GOLEMS
--------------------------
- Give a nitwit a sword or a bow (you need to be Trusted) and it becomes a Sentry.
  Sentries fight hostile mobs in their town and attack the town's Enemies. Archers shoot
  arrows from their pockets.
- A Sentry that follows a Hero becomes that Hero's bodyguard.
- Nitwit babies need a nitwit parent: 50% with one nitwit parent, 100% with two. Two
  normal villagers never have a nitwit. Spawn eggs give a nitwit 20% of the time.
- Iron golems get names like Rivet-42, join towns, and go after the town's Enemies.


COMMANDS
--------
For operators, or in single-player with cheats on. Tab completes town names, and
"nearest" picks the closest town.

  /town list
  /town reputation get <town> [players]
  /town reputation set <town> <players> <amount>
  /town reputation add <town> <players> <amount>
  /town reputation rank <town> <players> hero
  /town reload                      re-reads the settings file


SETTINGS
--------
The file config/townsfolk.properties is created the first time the game starts.
Set any option to false to turn that feature off (for example dailyTradeRerolls=false).
Every job, Sentries (weapons, range, damage, cooldowns, bodyguards), repairs, food, farm chests, Town Center generation, nitwit chances
and the reputation amounts can be changed. Use /town reload or restart to apply.


Township Expansion is still in development, so expect changes.
